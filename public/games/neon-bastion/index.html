🎯 Objective

Enemies march along the neon path toward the exit. Place towers on empty tiles to destroy them before they escape.

You start with 20 lives — each enemy that reaches the exit costs 1 life. Survive all 50 waves to win!

The map changes every 10 waves, and buildable tiles shift each wave — adapt your strategy!

🕹️ Controls

  • Tap a tower in the bottom bar to select it
  • Tap an empty tile to place it (costs gold)
  • Tap an existing tower to upgrade, sell, or change its targeting (Nearest / First / Last / Strongest)
  • Right-click or Escape to cancel placement
  • Spacebar or ▶ to send next wave early
  • F or the HUD button toggles speed (1× / 2× / 3×)
  • M or 🔊 mutes/unmutes audio

🗼 Towers

Blaster — 50g — Fast single-target damage
Frost Beam — 75g — Slows enemies by 40%
Plasma Cannon — 150g — Area splash damage
Sniper Turret — 200g — Long range, high damage
Tesla Coil — 300g — Chain-hits 3 enemies

Each tower can be upgraded 3 times for +50% damage per level.

💡 Tips

  • Enemy types resist differently: 🛡 Tanks shrug off single big hits but take extra from Plasma splash & Tesla chains. Runners resist slowing. Watch the badge on each enemy.
  • Frost Beams at chokepoints slow enemies for your damage dealers.
  • Save gold for boss waves — you also earn +5% interest on banked gold each wave (capped), so hoarding pays off.
  • Between waves, tiles outlined in red will vanish next wave — move towers off them before they're displaced.
  • Upgrade existing towers before spamming new ones.
  • Displaced towers are refunded 100%; manual sells refund 60%.
